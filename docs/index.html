Jump: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Jump is a tab switcher. To show you your tabs, it reads the titles and addresses of your open and recently closed tabs while its popup is open.

  • This information is used only to display and search your tabs inside the popup.
  • Nothing is stored. Jump keeps no history, logs, or settings on disk.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. Jump makes no network requests and contains no analytics, ads, or third-party code.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Favicons are loaded from Chrome's own local favicon cache, not from the websites themselves.
